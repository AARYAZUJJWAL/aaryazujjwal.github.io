Parametric Sloshing & Propellant Dynamics

Investigating sloshing instabilities in spacecraft propellant tanks via high-frequency excitation (25–300 Hz). Studying droplet ejection, interface instability, and gravity–capillary transitions using cryogenic (LN₂) and synthetic fluids.

High-Speed Helium Expansion Turbine

Design and performance analysis of helium expansion turbines for cryogenic systems at IPR Gandhinagar. Focused on rotor dynamics, bearing systems, thermodynamic optimization, and ultra-high vacuum environments.
